About.

I'm an AI Security Research Engineer who treats security like product: shipped, measured, and grounded in real systems. My day-to-day moves between adversarial ML, LLM-agent / MCP attack surface, and applied deep learning on hard, real-world data.

I trained as a researcher (M.Sc. in Intelligent Systems & Data Science) and ship as an engineer. I like clean experiments, calibrated metrics, and the quiet satisfaction of an attack that finally works — usually right after patching the model that was supposed to be safe.
