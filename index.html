About.

I'm an AI Security Research Engineer at Adversa AI, working at the intersection of adversarial ML, agentic AI security, and applied red-teaming. Day-to-day I turn lab-scale research and exploit discoveries into production-grade security features for enterprise AI systems - and design the countermeasures that hold up once the model meets real adversaries.

My focus areas: adversarial ML & threat research (evasion, data poisoning, model inversion); secure agentic architectures built on the Model Context Protocol with mitigations for prompt injection and logic-based privilege escalation; and applied AI red-teaming of LLMs and generative pipelines to surface emergent risks and "zero-day" behavioral flaws.

Before Adversa I co-founded WizeAssist and led a team of 7 shipping real-time pose-estimation and RAG-based LLM systems to production. I also research and defend at IAI Missiles & Space Division. Currently completing my M.Sc. in Intelligent Systems Engineering at Afeka, after a B.Sc. in Computer Science (AI/ML). Former combat first sergeant in Sayeret Nahal.

// flagship research · sheba medical center

Rectal-Cancer Classification on Endoscopic Ultrasound

An applied research project in collaboration with Prof. Dan Carter, a leading colorectal oncologist at Sheba Medical Center (Tel HaShomer). Built a deep-learning pipeline that automatically classifies rectal cancer from EUS images, reaching 91% sensitivity and 88% specificity on clinically validated datasets.

I independently proposed using Knowledge Distillation to compress Vision Transformers into lightweight CNNs - 4× smaller while preserving diagnostic performance. Added Attention Map Supervision on early conv layers for a +6% AUC bump on complex tumor cases. The model is the basis for a next-generation clinical decision-support tool.
